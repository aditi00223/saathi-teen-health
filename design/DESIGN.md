## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a warm, gentle, and confidential digital sanctuary for adolescent girls navigating puberty, menstrual health, emotional changes, and reproductive wellness. The aesthetic eliminates all clinical sterility, harsh institutional lines, and alarming medical motifs. Instead, it adopts an empathetic, tactile, and nurturing ambiance—resembling an intimate personal journal or an elder sister's trusted advice.

The visual direction combines **Soft Minimal Tactility** with **Pastel Comfort**:
- **Safety & Discretion:** Discreet visual cues, reassuring microcopy, and gentle contrast thresholds ensure that private health tracking feels protected and private.
- **Empowering Approachability:** Welcoming geometry, organic curvatures, and comfortable spaciousness eliminate fear and stigma around bodily changes.
- **Non-Clinical Clarity:** Health indicators prioritize calming reassurance over diagnostic anxiety. Alerts use soft warm peach and amber rather than alarming signal reds.

## Colors

The color palette is built upon soothing tones of lilac and whisper whites, anchored by grounding deep plum-slate neutrals and balanced with tranquil mint-teal accents.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas & Backdrops:** Whisper White (`#FAF9FD`) provides a warm, glare-free foundation. Elevated surface containers utilize Soft Lavender tints (`#EDE9FE`, `#DDD6FE`) and pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) to establish layered, readable hierarchy without harsh borders.
- **Primary Sanctuary Accent:** Rich Lavender-Violet (`#7C3AED`) delivers clear interactive hierarchy and focal points, softened by light lavender pills and gentle halos.
- **Reassurance & Wellness (Secondary):** Deep Mint Teal (`#0D9488`), Vibrant Sage-Teal (`#14B8A6`, `#2DD4BF`), and Mint Tint (`#CCFBF1`) represent balance, symptom relief, hygiene guidance, and self-care progress.
- **Text & Contrast:** Deep Slate-Plum (`#2E2A3B`) serves as primary copy for legibility that is softer on the eyes than pure black. Body secondary text uses Muted Lavender-Grey (`#6B6580`), maintaining AA/AAA contrast ratios against light violet and off-white backdrops.
- **Gentle Status Indicators:**
  - *Care/Urgent Advice:* Soft Pastel Peach (`#FB923C` on `#FFEDD5`) rather than alarming crimson.
  - *Caution/Notice:* Warm Amber Tinge (`#F59E0B` on `#FEF3C7`).
  - *Self-Care / Active Cycle Phase:* Serene Mint (`#0D9488` on `#CCFBF1`).

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly, humanist geometry, open apertures, and welcoming terminals. It feels approachable and warm, avoiding clinical coldness while retaining structured legibility across varied device viewports and entry-level smartphones.

- **Headlines:** Set with soft negative tracking and generous line heights to prevent high-density stress.
- **Body Copy:** Generous vertical spacing (`1.6x` line-height ratio on body levels) ensures effortless reading for educational guides, puberty FAQs, and symptom logs.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with medium weights to retain crisp contrast and legibility at smaller touch sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built with a responsive fluid-grid structure optimized primarily for private, hand-held mobile usage, adapting smoothly to tablet and desktop screens:
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Elements span the full container or split into 2-column touch-friendly cards. Tap targets never fall below 48px in height or width.
- **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout with 32px (`2rem`) margins and 20px gutters. Content remains centered within a readable reading container (max 720px width for sensitive text).
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout with a maximum centered container width of 1140px. Educational modules, tracking calendars, and chat spaces align into asymmetric, relaxed content blocks.

Spacious padding inside interactive elements ensures an unhurried, comfortable experience that reduces cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft, ambient depth that avoids sharp drops or harsh drop shadows.

- **Ambient Tinted Shadows:** Shadows utilize low opacity (4%–10%) tinted with deep plum-slate (`#2E2A3B`) rather than pure black, mixed with an imperceptible lavender halo (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.05)`).
- **Surface Elevation Levels:**
  - *Base Surface:* Flat Whisper White (`#FAF9FD`).
  - *Resting Cards & Containers:* Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border using `#EDE9FE` and a diffuse ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(46, 42, 59, 0.05)`.
  - *Floating Modals / Safe Drawer / Quick Discretion Bar:* Slightly elevated with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(46, 42, 59, 0.08)`.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Interactive inputs and state chips use light lavender borders (`1.5px solid #DDD6FE`) for definition that feels soft and tactile.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry. Organic, continuous curves express safety, protection, and gentle warmth.

- **Buttons & Chips:** True pill geometry (`9999px` radius) providing friendly, approachable tap affordances.
- **Standard Cards & Modals:** Deeply rounded boundaries (`1.5rem` to `2rem` / `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), eliminating sharp edges across all viewports.
- **Badges & Avatars:** Circular or stadium-pill capsules with soft interior padding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Fully pill-rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`), minimum height 48px, background in soothing violet (`#7C3AED`) with white text and a soft hover tint. Pressed state scales subtly down (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Deep Mint Teal (`#0D9488`) or Mint Tint (`#CCFBF1`) with dark teal text (`#0F766E`), offering a reassuring, gentle action.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Button:** Transparent background, Lavender-Grey text (`#6B6580`), gaining a soft lilac wash (`#EDE9FE`) on hover.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- Used for mood tags, physical symptoms (e.g., "Cramps", "Headache", "Energetic"), and topic filters.
- Default state: Lavender surface (`#F5F3FF`), border `1.5px solid #DDD6FE`, text `#4B465C`.
- Selected state: Deep Mint Teal (`#0D9488`) or Violet (`#7C3AED`) with white text and a faint glow shadow.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` or pale lilac (`#FDFCFE`), wrapped in a subtle border (`#EDE9FE`), and rounded to `24px` (`rounded-2xl`) or `32px` (`rounded-3xl`).
- Generous internal padding (20px to 24px) creates an unhurried, comfortable layout.

### Input Fields
- Generously padded (16px vertical, 20px horizontal) with rounded borders (`16px` to `9999px`).
- Border color `#DDD6FE` transitions on focus to a reassuring violet ring (`#7C3AED` at 20% opacity with `#7C3AED` stroke). Never red on neutral states.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Rounded checkboxes (8px radius) and circular radios with soft mint or violet fills when active, accompanied by smooth check transitions.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Discreet Quick-Exit Trigger:** Always-accessible, gentle floating pill or header control enabling immediate transition to a neutral study or weather screen.
- **Cycle & Symptom Orb:** A central, warm circular indicator displaying cycle phase with calming ambient gradients (lavender to soft mint) rather than countdown timers or clinical gauges.
- **Companion Advice Snippet:** A speech-bubble shaped card with a rounded avatar badge representing a friendly companion, featuring soft peach or mint reassurance backdrops.
